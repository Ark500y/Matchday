Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026
Privacy-First Design MATCHDAY is built from the ground up to operate offline with complete client-side data privacy. We do not transmit, sell, or monitor your personal or cricket match data.

1. Overview & Information Collection

MATCHDAY is a cricket scoring and scorecard generation tool. We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.

All data entered into the application—including squad rosters, team names, custom match rules, ball-by-ball actions, and bowler statistics—is stored solely on your local device.

2. Use of Local Storage (localStorage)

The app uses browser localStorage exclusively to:

  • Preserve active match progress so you can resume scoring if your browser is closed or refreshed.
  • Store default team rosters and role configurations (e.g. 30 SB squad).
  • Maintain custom match formats (overs per innings, bowler quotas).

This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to any remote server or cloud database.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

MATCHDAY does not use tracking cookies, advertising beacons, or third-party analytic SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel). We do not profile users or monitor usage habits.

4. PDF Generation & Document Export

When you use the "Download Scorecard PDF" feature, the scorecard document is rendered directly within your web browser using standard browser printing APIs (window.print). No match summaries or generated PDF documents are transmitted over the internet.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

You have full autonomy over all information stored within the app:

  • Clicking "New Match" resets match records and delivery history.
  • Clearing your web browser's cache or website data immediately removes all stored rosters and scoring history from your device.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new capabilities or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
